Deltatale

A fan-made Undertale & Deltarune boss rush. Pick a boss, fight, retry.

Choose a boss.

Controls

Arrows / WASD – move the SOUL, navigate menus
Z / Enter – confirm, shoot (yellow SOUL)
X / Shift – back
Esc / P – pause, M – sound

SOUL modes

♥ Red – move freely. ♥ Blue – gravity, jump with the key opposite to gravity. ♥ Green – can't move, turn the shield. ♥ Yellow – Z shoots (hold for BIG SHOT in Deltarune). ♥ Purple – move along the strings. Blue attacks hurt only while you move, orange ones only while you stand still.

About

Deltatale is an unofficial fan project. Undertale and Deltarune are by Toby Fox; this project is not affiliated with them. Boss art here is drawn in code as fan-made approximations.
