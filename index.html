Controls

WASD / arrows – move the SOUL, menus
Z / Enter – confirm (and shoot), X / Shift – back
Esc / P – pause, M – sound

SOUL modes

RED moves freely. BLUE has gravity – ↑ jumps. GREEN can't move – turn the shield towards the spears. YELLOW shoots with Z (hold for a BIG SHOT in Deltarune). PURPLE is stuck on strings.

Bullets

Cyan attacks only hurt you while you move – stand still. Orange attacks only hurt you while you stand still – keep moving. Flashing red areas are about to be hit.

Art & music

Bosses without art show a placeholder card. Put your own sprites and music in assets/bosses/<id>/ and list them in assets/bosses/manifest.js.
